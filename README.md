# The Complete Pianist

A free, self-paced path for teaching yourself piano.

**[piano-guide.delfost.workers.dev](https://piano-guide.delfost.workers.dev)**

Self-taught players rarely stall for lack of effort. They stall because the advice they collect never forms a sequence. The Complete Pianist puts it in order: relaxed technique first, then sight-reading once the hands are calm, with the two growing side by side, level by level.

It is a shortlist, not a catalogue. Each step names the few books, scores and videos that experienced players and teachers recommend most, says what to do with them, and tells you how to know when you are done.

## What it does

- **Gives you a path.** Two technique stages, then Pre-Staff and Levels 0 to 7 of sight-reading. Every level has its resources, a checklist, and benchmark pieces to test yourself against. A harmony track (lead sheets, comping, jazz harmony) branches off along the way, and separate pages cover reading music, rhythm, keyboard geometry and playing by ear.
- **Keeps track of where you are.** Tick off tasks and resources as you finish them. Each level shows its percentage and the introduction shows the whole curriculum. Your progress is saved in your own browser, so there is no account to create.
- **Lets you make it yours.** Swap in an alternative method book, and choose which benchmark piece you want to be measured by.
- **Shows its sources.** Claims about how learning and the body work cite numbered studies in the reference library.
- **Stays open to correction.** Every page ends with an *Edit page* link. Anyone with a GitHub account can fix or add something from the browser and send it for review. The Contributing page of the site explains how.

## Free

The Complete Pianist is free and always will be: no account, no paywall. The resources it points to are labelled, so you can see which are free and which cost money.
